---
title: All Means All
description: "Form & Fury is a safe, celebratory home for all women in sport."
---

In this era of bitter divides and performative neutrality, *Form & Fury* unapologetically takes a stand for what matters. We don't just cover women's sports—we champion the full spectrum of women who make those sports powerful, thrilling, and worth fighting for. In a media landscape that still sidelines, erases, or tokenizes women's athletic achievements, we create a space that is unapologetically for women—cis, trans, queer, disabled, Black, Indigenous, and every intersection between.

*Form & Fury* is a safe, celebratory home for *all* women. Trans women are women. That's not up for debate here, and our platform will never entertain hate disguised as "concern" or exclusion masked as "science." Before anyone attempts to argue "biology," let's be clear: biological sex is not binary. There are more than two chromosomal patterns, more than two hormone profiles, and more than two bodies—and all of them belong in the conversation when it comes to equity and access in sport.

We believe inclusion isn't just a checkbox—it's a muscle. It takes effort, intention, and repetition to build a truly inclusive media platform. That means amplifying voices historically excluded, showing up when it's inconvenient, and standing firm when the backlash comes. *Form & Fury* is here to tell the stories, show the grit, and back the athletes who deserve the spotlight—not in spite of who they are, but because of it.
