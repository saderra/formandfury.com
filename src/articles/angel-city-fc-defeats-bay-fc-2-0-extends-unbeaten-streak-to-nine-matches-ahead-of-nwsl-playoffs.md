Angel City FC continued its remarkable late-season momentum on Saturday, October 24, 2026, securing a commanding 2-0 victory over Northern California rival Bay FC at BMO Stadium in Los Angeles. In front of an enthusiastic crowd of 20,548 supporters, Angel City delivered another important performance as the National Women's Soccer League regular season approached its conclusion. Second-half goals from Gisele Thompson and Riley Tiernan proved decisive, while goalkeeper Angelina Anderson recorded another clean sheet. The victory extended Angel City's unbeaten streak to nine consecutive matches and moved the team into fourth place in the NWSL standings with 49 points, strengthening its opportunity to host a postseason match.



The evening carried additional significance for Angel City, which had already secured its first NWSL playoff appearance since 2023. Entering the match following a thrilling 3-2 comeback victory over Denver Summit FC, Angel City looked to maintain its momentum and build confidence before the postseason. Under interim head coach Leif Smerud, the club has demonstrated growing consistency, resilience, and attacking efficiency during the closing months of the campaign. The atmosphere at BMO Stadium reflected the importance of the occasion, with supporters celebrating Fan Appreciation Night while watching their team pursue another victory in what has become one of the most encouraging stretches in club history.



Angel City established an attacking presence almost immediately after kickoff, applying pressure to Bay FC's defensive organization. Taylor Suarez tested goalkeeper Jordan Silkowitz in the opening minute, signaling the home side's intention to control the contest. Midfielder Ary Borges nearly opened the scoring in the third minute, directing a close-range header narrowly wide of the left post. Angel City continued to dominate possession and create opportunities throughout the opening half, with Evelyn Shores forcing another important save from Silkowitz in the 34th minute. Despite controlling much of the attacking play, Angel City could not find the breakthrough before halftime, sending both teams into the locker rooms locked in a scoreless draw.



The breakthrough finally arrived in the 49th minute, just four minutes after the restart. Midfielder Maiara Niehues, introduced as a halftime substitute, delivered a well-timed through ball to Gisele Thompson, who advanced into the right side of the penalty area and finished confidently into the upper-right corner. The goal gave Angel City a deserved 1-0 advantage and marked Thompson's fifth goal of the season. Her performance provided another example of the young defender's increasing influence on the team's attacking production. Thompson's combination of speed, technical ability, and composure has made her an increasingly important contributor throughout Angel City's impressive run of form.



With the lead established, Angel City continued pushing forward rather than settling into a defensive approach. Shores threatened with a header that sailed over the crossbar in the 54th minute, while Silkowitz produced another important save shortly afterward to prevent the hosts from doubling their advantage. Ally Sentnor came close to scoring around the hour mark after receiving a dangerous delivery from Suarez, but her attempt went over the crossbar. Icelandic international Sveindís Jónsdóttir also tested the Bay FC goalkeeper in the 70th minute. Although Angel City repeatedly created opportunities, Silkowitz remained one of Bay FC's standout performers, finishing the evening with six saves and keeping the visitors within striking distance for much of the contest.



Bay FC struggled to establish consistent attacking possession against Angel City's organized defensive structure. The visitors entered halftime without registering a shot and found limited opportunities to challenge Anderson after the break. Despite occasional moments of promising buildup, Bay FC could not turn possession into a decisive scoring opportunity. Head coach Emma Coates acknowledged afterward that her team had struggled to match the tempo of the contest. Nevertheless, one encouraging moment arrived late in the match when veteran defender Emily Menges returned to competitive action for the first time since April 2025 following maternity leave, marking an important personal milestone during a difficult evening for the visitors.



Angel City finally secured its second goal in the 83rd minute through substitute Riley Tiernan. Midfielder Nealy Martin, who had entered during the second half, delivered a sliding pass into a dangerous position inside the penalty area. Tiernan capitalized on the opportunity, finishing from close range to double the advantage and effectively put the contest beyond Bay FC's reach. The goal was Tiernan's second of the season and illustrated the impact Angel City's substitutes have provided during its late-season surge. Both Martin and Tiernan entered the match after halftime, reinforcing the team's attacking depth and demonstrating the importance of contributions throughout the roster during a demanding NWSL campaign.



Defensively, Angel City produced another disciplined performance, with captain Sarah Gorden helping organize a back line that limited Bay FC's opportunities throughout the evening. Anderson completed her 10th clean sheet of the season, another significant achievement in a campaign defined by the team's improving balance between attacking creativity and defensive stability. The victory also brought Angel City to 50 goals for the season for the first time in club history, underscoring the development of an increasingly productive attacking unit. With 14 wins, eight losses, and seven draws, Angel City has positioned itself as a competitive postseason contender, combining emerging young talent with experienced leadership and a growing sense of confidence.



Angel City now turns its attention to the final regular-season match against Boston Legacy FC on Sunday, November 1, at Dignity Health Sports Park, where another positive result could help secure a home playoff match. Bay FC, meanwhile, will conclude its season against San Diego Wave FC at PayPal Park on the same afternoon. For Angel City, Saturday's victory represented considerably more than three points. It offered another demonstration of the team's progress, strengthened its postseason ambitions, and gave supporters additional reasons for optimism heading into November. With nine consecutive matches unbeaten, an attack capable of producing decisive moments, and an increasingly confident defensive unit, Angel City approaches the NWSL playoffs carrying some of its strongest momentum of the 2026 season.
