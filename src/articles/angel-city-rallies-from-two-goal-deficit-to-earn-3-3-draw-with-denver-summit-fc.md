Angel City FC and Denver Summit FC produced a six-goal thriller Friday night, September 11, 2026, battling to a 3-3 draw at BMO Stadium in Los Angeles. The first meeting between the two clubs arrived with both sides fighting for position in a crowded NWSL playoff race, and the match delivered the urgency expected from two teams separated by little in the standings.



Angel City struck first just before halftime. Defender Sarah Gorden won possession and quickly moved the ball forward to Maiara Niehues, who finished in the 45th minute to give the hosts a 1-0 advantage. The goal was Niehues' eighth of the season, matching Angel City's single-season club record and sending ACFC into the break with momentum.



That advantage disappeared almost immediately after play resumed. Denver's Yuzuki Yamamoto equalized in the 49th minute with a finish from inside the penalty area, scoring for a third consecutive match. Just one minute later, Lindsey Heaps capitalized on a rebound to put the Summit ahead 2-1, recording her first goal for Denver and completely changing the direction of the contest.



Denver's second-half surge continued in the 61st minute when Janine Sonis struck from distance to extend the lead to 3-1. After entering halftime behind, the Summit had scored three times in roughly 12 minutes and appeared positioned to leave Los Angeles with a crucial road victory.



Angel City responded almost immediately. Sveindís Jónsdóttir broke forward and delivered the ball across for Prisca Chilufya, who finished from close range in the 63rd minute. The play was initially ruled offside, but a video review overturned the decision and awarded the goal, cutting Denver's advantage to 3-2 and bringing BMO Stadium back to life.



The comeback was completed in the 74th minute. Denver defender Ayo Oke was penalized after a high boot caught Ally Sentnor in the face inside the penalty area, giving Angel City a chance to level from the spot. Sentnor stepped forward and converted the penalty to make it 3-3, completing ACFC's recovery from a two-goal deficit.



Both teams continued searching for a winner over the closing stages, but neither could produce the decisive seventh goal. Angel City had experienced a dramatic swing from leading 1-0 to trailing 3-1 before recovering, while Denver was left to reflect on a two-goal advantage that disappeared in little more than 10 minutes.



The draw moved Angel City to a 9-8-6 record, while Denver Summit finished the night at 8-8-7. With both clubs pursuing a place among the NWSL's top eight, sharing the points did little to separate two teams that entered the match directly competing for postseason positioning.



For Angel City, the night ultimately became a demonstration of resilience. Denver's three-goal burst could have decided the match, but Chilufya's response and Sentnor's penalty ensured ACFC came away with something from an unpredictable first encounter between the clubs. Denver, meanwhile, showed again that its attack can create goals quickly, setting the stage for an intriguing rematch later in the season.
