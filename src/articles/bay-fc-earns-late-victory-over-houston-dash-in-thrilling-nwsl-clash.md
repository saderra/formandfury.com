Bay FC and the Houston Dash delivered one of the most entertaining matches of the NWSL season on August 2, with the California side pulling off a 3-2 comeback win at PayPal Park. In a game defined by momentum swings and late drama, Bay FC continued their strong run of form, while Houston was left to rue missed chances and defensive lapses.

The Dash started brightly, opening the scoring in the 14th minute through forward Michelle Alozie, who capitalized on a defensive error. Bay FC responded with high pressure and equalized before halftime thanks to Asisat Oshoala’s precise finish from the edge of the box. The pace of the game remained relentless, with both sides pushing forward in search of a second goal.

Houston regained the lead early in the second half when María Sánchez curled a stunning free kick into the top corner. However, Bay FC didn’t fold. Their midfield trio took control of possession, and substitute Scarlett Camberos brought fresh energy, eventually assisting the equalizer in the 78th minute scored by Alex Loera.

With the score tied and stoppage time winding down, Bay FC found their winner. A corner kick in the 92nd minute led to a chaotic scramble in the box, and it was forward Deyna Castellanos who slotted home the game-winning goal. The crowd erupted, and the players mobbed Castellanos in celebration.

The win marks a crucial three points for Bay FC as they chase a playoff spot, while Houston remains mired in the bottom half of the table. Both teams showed resilience and attacking intent, but it was Bay FC’s late surge that proved decisive on a night to remember in San Jose.
