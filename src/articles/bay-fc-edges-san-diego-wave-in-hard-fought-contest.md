Bay FC and the San Diego Wave delivered an intense matchup at PayPal Park, with both teams battling for possession and field position from the opening whistle. The pace was fast and physical, with each side pressing high and forcing turnovers.

The Wave created several early chances, driven by their midfield control and wide play. However, Bay FC’s defense held firm, with goalkeeper Katelyn Rowland making key stops to keep the game level in the first half.

Momentum shifted in the second half when Bay FC found the breakthrough. A well-placed cross into the box was converted by a clinical finish, sending the home fans into celebration and putting the hosts up 1-0.

San Diego pushed hard for an equalizer, pressing higher up the field and nearly breaking through on multiple occasions. Despite their efforts, Bay FC’s defensive organization and counterattacks kept the Wave at bay.

In the final minutes, Bay FC held strong under pressure to secure the victory. The result adds an important boost to their season, while the Wave will look to regroup quickly with the playoff race intensifying.
