LAFC dominated the attacking numbers but needed a late goal from Denis Bouanga to earn a 1-1 draw against the Portland Timbers on Saturday, August 22, 2026, at BMO Stadium. After falling behind in the opening minutes, the Black & Gold spent nearly the entire evening chasing the match and repeatedly testing a Portland defense that refused to break until the closing stages.

Portland stunned the home crowd just four minutes into the contest. Kristoffer Velde intercepted an errant pass near midfield and quickly drove toward the LAFC defense before slipping the ball through to David Da Costa. Da Costa met the pass first time and sent his shot beyond goalkeeper Hugo Lloris to give the Timbers an early 1-0 advantage.

The goal sparked an immediate response from LAFC. Bouanga, Mathieu Choinière and Yevhen Cheberko were among those who threatened as the hosts began piling pressure on Portland goalkeeper James Pantemis. LAFC continually moved the match toward the Timbers’ penalty area, but Pantemis and the visitors’ defense kept the Black & Gold from turning their territorial advantage into an equalizer.

By halftime, LAFC had produced a club-record 21 shots in the opening 45 minutes yet remained behind 1-0. The extraordinary shooting volume illustrated how one-sided stretches of the match had become, but it also highlighted LAFC’s difficulties in front of goal. Pantemis made five first-half saves, while Portland survived several additional attempts that went wide or were blocked before reaching him.

The pattern continued after the break. LAFC controlled possession in dangerous areas and kept searching for a way through, while Portland protected its narrow advantage and looked for opportunities to counter. The Timbers’ resistance forced LAFC to remain patient even as the shot total climbed toward another club record and the possibility of a third consecutive league match without a goal became increasingly real.

The breakthrough finally arrived in the 79th minute. Jude Terry found Bouanga on the left side, and the LAFC forward cut between two Portland defenders before sending a right-footed strike from outside the penalty area into the net. Bouanga’s team-leading 11th MLS goal of the season tied the match at 1-1 and ended LAFC’s 312-minute league scoring drought.

LAFC continued pushing for all three points after drawing level. Bouanga remained at the center of the attack, while Ryan Hollingshead also came close to producing a winning goal during the closing minutes. Bouanga finished with a career-high 12 shots as LAFC totaled a club-record 34 attempts, compared with nine for Portland, but Pantemis completed an outstanding night with 10 saves.

Portland’s task became even more difficult during the final minutes when substitute Vincent Janssen was sent off. Janssen received a yellow card in the 89th minute and then picked up another in third-minute stoppage time, reducing the Timbers to 10 men. LAFC continued pressing, and Pantemis was called upon once more to deny Bouanga during the final moments and preserve the draw for Portland.

The result ended LAFC’s two-match losing streak but also left the Black & Gold with the feeling that more had been available after such a dominant attacking performance. LAFC moved to 10 wins, seven losses and five draws for 35 points, remaining third in the Western Conference. Portland improved to 8-9-4 and departed Los Angeles with a valuable road point, while LAFC turned its attention to an August 29 visit to D.C. United after finally rediscovering its scoring touch.
