Dallas Trinity FC delivered a memorable performance on Saturday, October 17, 2026, defeating Mexican powerhouse Chivas de Guadalajara Femenil 3-2 in the second annual State Fair Clásico at the historic Cotton Bowl in Dallas, Texas. Played during the closing weekend of the State Fair of Texas, the international friendly brought together two clubs representing different leagues and soccer cultures in a celebration of women's professional soccer. Dallas established a commanding two-goal advantage before Chivas mounted an impressive second-half comeback, setting the stage for a dramatic stoppage-time winner from Jasmine Hamid. The victory marked an important milestone for Trinity, giving the club its first international win and another unforgettable moment in front of its home supporters.



The atmosphere surrounding the Cotton Bowl reflected the significance of the occasion, with supporters of both teams gathering at Fair Park for an afternoon of soccer and State Fair festivities. The matchup offered Dallas Trinity, which competes in the Gainbridge Super League, an opportunity to test itself against one of the most recognizable clubs in Liga MX Femenil. Chivas arrived with a respected reputation in Mexican women's soccer and a passionate following that extended well beyond Guadalajara. For Dallas, the international showcase represented another opportunity to strengthen its identity, attract new audiences, and demonstrate the growing popularity of professional women's soccer throughout North Texas.



Dallas began the contest with confidence, applying attacking pressure and finding opportunities to exploit space along the wings. The home side's movement and willingness to deliver dangerous crosses created difficulties for the Guadalajara defense throughout the opening half. Trinity broke through in the 29th minute, taking a 1-0 lead following an attacking move down the right flank. Sixteen-year-old Kaiya Brewer, making her professional debut, played a prominent role in the breakthrough, with contemporary match reports identifying her as the scorer, although Chivas' official report credited Drew Coomans. Regardless of the scoring discrepancy, the opening goal rewarded Dallas for its aggressive approach and provided an early advantage against a talented opponent.



The home side continued to attack rather than settling for its narrow lead, and that determination produced another goal before halftime. In the 41st minute, Juliet "LJ" Moore doubled the advantage with a close-range finish after another dangerous attacking sequence developed along the right side. The similarities between Trinity's two first-half goals highlighted the effectiveness of its wide attacking play and exposed the challenges facing the Chivas defensive unit. Dallas carried a 2-0 advantage into the halftime break, having capitalized on its opportunities while limiting Guadalajara's ability to establish consistent attacking momentum. The result at the interval reflected a strong opening performance from the Texas club, but the visitors were far from finished.



Chivas responded after halftime with several personnel changes designed to improve its attacking effectiveness. Among the most influential introductions was veteran forward Alicia Cervantes, whose presence immediately strengthened Guadalajara's offensive threat. The visitors began to establish more possession in dangerous areas, forcing Dallas to defend deeper and respond to an increasingly determined opponent. Their persistence paid off in the 66th minute when Cervantes produced an impressive finish to cut the deficit to 2-1. The goal energized the Chivas supporters inside the Cotton Bowl and completely changed the complexion of the match, transforming what had appeared to be a comfortable Dallas advantage into a tense contest.



The momentum continued to shift toward Guadalajara as the second half progressed, with Cervantes once again proving decisive in front of goal. In the 75th minute, a shot from Jasmine Casarez struck the crossbar, leaving the ball loose inside the penalty area. Cervantes reacted quickly, converting the rebound to complete her brace and level the score at 2-2. Her second goal completed Chivas' comeback from a two-goal deficit and demonstrated why she remains such an important attacking figure for the Mexican club. Dallas suddenly faced the possibility of surrendering a match it had controlled for much of the opening half, while Guadalajara appeared capable of completing an extraordinary turnaround.



With the score tied heading into the closing minutes, both clubs searched for an opportunity to claim victory. Dallas attempted to regain the attacking rhythm that had been so effective earlier in the afternoon, while Chivas looked to capitalize on the confidence generated by its second-half resurgence. The final stages became increasingly tense as each defensive challenge, attacking movement, and loose ball carried greater significance. Although the match was classified as an international friendly and did not count toward either club's league standings, the competitive intensity suggested that both sides were determined to finish the afternoon with a victory. Supporters remained engaged as the contest moved into stoppage time with the result still uncertain.



The decisive moment finally arrived in the 92nd minute when Jasmine Hamid capitalized on a defensive mistake by Guadalajara to score the winning goal for Dallas Trinity. Her late finish restored the home side's advantage at 3-2 and sparked celebrations among the Dallas players and supporters. After watching their two-goal lead disappear during a challenging second half, Trinity demonstrated resilience by finding one final attacking opportunity when it mattered most. Hamid's intervention ensured that Cervantes' impressive individual performance would not be enough to rescue a result for Chivas. The dramatic conclusion provided the perfect ending to a five-goal encounter filled with attacking quality, momentum changes, and memorable individual contributions.



For Dallas Trinity FC, the 3-2 victory represented more than an entertaining result against international opposition. The club demonstrated its attacking potential, its ability to compete with an established Mexican powerhouse, and its determination to respond under pressure. For Chivas, the defeat came despite a spirited comeback and a standout performance from Cervantes, offering valuable lessons as the team prepared to return to Liga MX Femenil competition. The second annual State Fair Clásico once again highlighted the cultural and sporting connections between Texas and Mexico while creating another memorable occasion for women's soccer at the Cotton Bowl. With Hamid's stoppage-time winner securing a historic victory, Dallas Trinity closed the afternoon with a statement performance that will be remembered as one of the defining moments of its growing international identity.
