Everton FC welcomed Brighton FC to Goodison Park on January 23rd, 2026, with both sides eager to gain momentum in the Barclays Women’s Super League. Played under the floodlights at England’s iconic venue, the match carried a sense of occasion, with Everton looking to capitalize on home support and Brighton aiming to continue their steady league progress.

The opening stages were played at a high tempo, with Everton pressing aggressively and Brighton responding through quick transitions. Chances were limited early on, but the physicality in midfield set the tone, as neither side was willing to concede control. The Toffees looked particularly dangerous down the flanks, while Brighton remained compact and disciplined at the back.

As the match settled, Brighton began to assert themselves in possession, stringing together patient passing sequences that tested Everton’s defensive shape. Everton, however, showed resilience, defending deep when required and threatening on the counterattack. Goalkeepers at both ends were called into action before the break, keeping the contest finely balanced.

The second half brought renewed urgency, with Everton pushing higher up the pitch and Brighton responding with increased attacking intent. Set pieces became a key feature, and the atmosphere inside Goodison Park intensified as both teams sensed an opportunity to tilt the game in their favor. Despite sustained pressure, clear-cut chances remained scarce.

In the closing stages, fatigue set in but commitment never dropped, as both sides fought until the final whistle. The result reflected the evenly matched nature of the contest, earning Everton and Brighton a valuable point each in their respective league campaigns and leaving supporters appreciative of a competitive and disciplined Women’s Super League display.
