Lucie Havlickova of Czechia stepped into the spotlight at the Ostrava Open on February 3, 2026, taking on Britain’s Katie Boulter in front of an energized home audience. Playing on familiar hard courts in Ostrava, Havlickova carried both expectation and excitement, embracing the moment against one of the tour’s more established competitors.

From the opening games, Havlickova demonstrated her intent, striking cleanly off both wings and looking to dictate rallies early. Her movement around the baseline and willingness to step inside the court reflected a player eager to test herself at WTA level, while the crowd responded warmly to every aggressive foray and well-timed winner.

Boulter, known for her powerful serve and flat groundstrokes, brought a contrasting style that challenged the young Czech. The Briton’s experience showed in longer exchanges, where she absorbed pace and looked to exploit openings with depth and precision, forcing Havlickova to stay sharp and composed under pressure.

As the match unfolded, momentum shifted back and forth, highlighting the competitive balance between youthful ambition and seasoned consistency. Havlickova’s resilience stood out as she continued to fight for every point, drawing loud support from the stands and feeding off the atmosphere inside the arena.

Regardless of the final outcome, the contest marked another valuable chapter in Havlickova’s development, offering a glimpse of her potential on home soil against high-caliber opposition. For Ostrava fans, it was a memorable afternoon that underscored the emergence of a local talent unafraid of the WTA spotlight.
