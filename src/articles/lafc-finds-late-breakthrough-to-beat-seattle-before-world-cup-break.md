LAFC closed the pre-World Cup portion of its MLS schedule with the kind of result that can reshape a team’s mood in a single night, beating the Seattle Sounders 1-0 on May 24, 2026, at BMO Stadium. In a tight Western Conference matchup defined by patience, pressure, and late drama, the Black & Gold found their breakthrough in the final minutes and protected it through Seattle’s closing push.

The match was scoreless for most of the night, but LAFC carried long stretches of attacking intent. The home side generated the better volume of chances early, testing Seattle’s defensive shape and forcing the Sounders to absorb pressure while looking for opportunities to break forward. LAFC’s front line stayed active, with Son Heung-Min, Denis Bouanga, and David Martínez helping stretch the field and keep Seattle’s back line under stress.

Seattle, meanwhile, remained dangerous enough to keep the match tense. The Sounders found moments through Jordan Morris, Albert Rusnák, and Cristian Roldan, particularly as the game opened up after halftime. But LAFC goalkeeper Thomas Hasal, starting again in place of the injured Hugo Lloris, delivered a composed performance and made the saves needed to keep the visitors off the board.

The decisive moment arrived in the 86th minute, when substitute Tyler Boyd delivered a cross into the box and Timothy Tillman slipped into position at the back post. Tillman finished the chance for the only goal of the night, giving LAFC the lead after a long stretch of frustration in front of goal. The strike was especially meaningful for Tillman, who was making his 150th appearance for the club in all competitions.

Tillman’s goal rewarded LAFC’s persistence. The Black & Gold had outshot Seattle and created enough pressure to make the late winner feel earned, even if it took nearly the full match to arrive. For a team trying to halt a difficult run, the finish supplied both relief and a reminder of how valuable late-game execution can be in conference play.

Hasal’s role was just as important. Seattle pushed for an equalizer in the final minutes, sending numbers forward and creating set-piece danger, but LAFC stayed organized in front of its goalkeeper. Hasal finished with five saves and secured his first shutout of the season, helping LAFC post another clean sheet in what has been a strong defensive campaign.

The victory snapped LAFC’s three-game losing streak and moved the club to 7 wins, 5 losses, and 3 draws in MLS play, good for 24 points. It also lifted LAFC into fifth place in the Western Conference, giving the team a more positive position heading into Major League Soccer’s extended pause for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

For Seattle, the defeat continued a challenging stretch after a strong unbeaten run earlier in the season. The Sounders remained competitive throughout the night, but they could not turn their late chances into a point. In a match where margins were thin, LAFC’s one clean look at the end proved to be the difference.

LAFC will now enter the break with a needed boost before returning to action in July. The Black & Gold’s next league match is scheduled for July 17 on the road against the LA Galaxy, a rivalry game that will immediately test whether the momentum from this late win can carry into the next phase of the season.
