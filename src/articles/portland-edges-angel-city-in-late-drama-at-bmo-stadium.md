Angel City FC and Portland Thorns FC delivered the kind of late-game tension that defines a growing NWSL rivalry, with Portland leaving Los Angeles with a 2-1 victory after a wild finish at BMO Stadium.

For much of the match, Angel City worked to impose its rhythm at home, trying to use possession, width, and pressure from the crowd to unsettle a Portland side comfortable absorbing pressure and waiting for chances to break forward.

Portland’s breakthrough came through Pietra Tordin, whose strong start to the 2026 season continued with another important goal. Her finish gave the Thorns the cushion they needed in a match where space was limited and every attacking sequence carried weight.

Angel City pushed for a response, leaning into urgency as the second half wore on. The hosts created moments of pressure around the Portland penalty area, but the Thorns remained organized enough to keep ACFC chasing the match.

The decisive moment arrived deep into stoppage time, when Sophia Wilson scored on a counterattack after an Angel City free kick attempt. The goal marked an important personal moment for Wilson, putting her back on the scoresheet and giving Portland what appeared to be a secure 2-0 lead.

Angel City, however, refused to let the match end quietly. Just minutes later, Prisca Chilufya finished a one-time volley at the right post after a cross from Emily Sams, cutting the deficit to 2-1 and reigniting the home crowd.

That late goal gave ACFC one final burst of belief, but Portland managed the remaining moments well enough to protect the result. The Thorns’ composure in the closing stretch proved just as important as their finishing.

For Portland, the win represented a valuable road result and another sign of sharpness in the early-season table picture. The Thorns showed balance, taking their chances while handling long spells of pressure away from home.

For Angel City, the defeat was frustrating but not without positives. The late push, especially the connection between Sams and Chilufya, offered evidence of the fight and attacking quality ACFC can build on as the season continues.
