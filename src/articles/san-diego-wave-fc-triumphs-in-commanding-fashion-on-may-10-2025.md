San Diego Wave FC lit up Snapdragon Stadium on Saturday night with a statement 3-0 win over the Houston Dash, maintaining their unbeaten home record and energizing a passionate crowd of over 25,000. The victory marks the club’s third consecutive win and vaults them into second place in the league table.

Forward Alex Morgan led the charge with a clinical brace, scoring in the 22nd and 57th minutes. Her first goal came from a slick one-touch finish off a cross from Jaedyn Shaw, while her second was a composed breakaway finish that left the goalkeeper no chance. Morgan’s sharp movement and leadership were on full display throughout the match.

The midfield trio of Danielle Colaprico, Savannah McCaskill, and Taylor Kornieck dominated possession and tempo, consistently breaking up Houston’s attempts to counterattack and launching crisp forward passes. Colaprico, in particular, earned Player of the Match honors for her tireless defensive work and intelligent distribution.

Goalkeeper Kailen Sheridan made several crucial saves to preserve the clean sheet, including a diving stop in the 65th minute that snuffed out Houston’s best scoring opportunity. Sheridan’s communication and command of the box played a major role in the defense’s strong showing.

Head coach Casey Stoney praised her squad’s cohesion and intensity after the match. “We’ve been building week by week, and tonight was a complete team performance. I’m proud of how we executed our game plan and kept our focus for 90 minutes,” Stoney said in the post-game press conference.

The win was also notable for the debut of 18-year-old defender Olivia Moultrie, who came on in the final 15 minutes and showed poise beyond her years. Her inclusion signals Stoney’s commitment to youth development and squad depth as the season intensifies.

With the victory, San Diego Wave FC continues to build momentum as they head into a tough stretch of games, including a midweek clash with Portland Thorns FC. Fans and analysts alike are starting to believe this team has the depth and quality to make a serious run at the NWSL Championship.
