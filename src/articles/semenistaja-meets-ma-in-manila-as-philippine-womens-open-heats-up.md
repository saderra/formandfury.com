Darja Semenistaja of Latvia and Ma Yexin of China square off in the women’s singles round of 32 at the WTA Philippine Women’s Open in Manila, the Philippines, on Jan. 27, 2026. The matchup brings together two players at different stages of their careers, each seeking momentum early in the tournament on the tropical hard courts.

Semenistaja arrives in Manila looking to translate her recent tour experience into a deep run, leaning on her baseline consistency and ability to absorb pressure. Known for constructing points patiently, the Latvian will aim to dictate rallies and capitalize on any openings created by extended exchanges.

Ma, meanwhile, represents a new wave of Chinese talent eager to make an impact on the WTA stage. Her game is built around speed and aggressive court coverage, attributes that can thrive in quick conditions. For Ma, the round of 32 presents an opportunity to test herself against a seasoned opponent and announce her presence at this level.

The Philippine Women’s Open has quickly established itself as a key stop on the calendar, offering valuable ranking points and a competitive international field. Manila’s enthusiastic crowds and warm conditions add an extra layer of challenge, often rewarding players who adapt fastest both physically and tactically.

With a place in the round of 16 on the line, the contest promises a blend of tactical discipline and youthful ambition. Whether Semenistaja’s experience prevails or Ma’s energy breaks through, the encounter is set to be an intriguing chapter in the early rounds of the tournament.
