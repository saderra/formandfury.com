The Seattle Storm earned a gritty road victory against the Minnesota Lynx, relying on a balanced attack and defensive pressure to come away with the win. Both teams entered the game battling for playoff positioning, and the intensity was evident from the opening tip.

Seattle controlled the tempo early with a strong start from their backcourt, who pushed the pace and created open looks in transition. The Lynx countered with physical inside play, but the Storm’s defense forced key turnovers that helped them maintain a narrow edge.

Minnesota fought back in the second half, led by sharp perimeter shooting and aggressive rebounding that cut into Seattle’s lead. The Lynx briefly held momentum late in the third quarter, energizing the home crowd and setting up a dramatic final frame.

In the closing minutes, the Storm’s veteran leadership proved crucial. They executed well in half-court sets, knocking down contested shots and hitting free throws under pressure to keep Minnesota at bay. Their ability to close out possessions with rebounds sealed the outcome.

The victory gave Seattle another confidence boost as they continue their push toward the postseason. For the Lynx, the loss was a setback but also a reminder of the adjustments needed to stay competitive in the playoff race.
