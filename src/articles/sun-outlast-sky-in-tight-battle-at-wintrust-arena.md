The Connecticut Sun and Chicago Sky clashed in a spirited matchup at Wintrust Arena, with both teams fighting to gain ground in the playoff race. The energy inside the building matched the intensity on the court, as neither side was willing to give an inch through much of the evening.

Chicago opened strong behind efficient shooting from their backcourt, moving the ball well and knocking down key perimeter shots. The Sky’s pace and execution kept Connecticut on its heels early, but the Sun’s defensive adjustments allowed them to close the gap before halftime.

In the third quarter, Connecticut began to assert themselves in the paint, using their size and physicality to generate second-chance opportunities. Alyssa Thomas’s all-around effort provided a steadying force, while DeWanna Bonner delivered timely scoring to help swing momentum in favor of the Sun.

The Sky refused to go quietly, responding with runs of their own led by their dynamic guard play. Marina Mabrey and Chennedy Carter sparked a surge in the fourth quarter, bringing the home crowd to its feet and setting up a tense finish down the stretch.

Ultimately, Connecticut’s composure in crunch time proved decisive. Executing their sets with precision and hitting free throws when it mattered most, the Sun held off the late push from Chicago to walk away with a hard-fought victory on the road.
