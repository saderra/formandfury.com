The Golden State Valkyries and Los Angeles Sparks faced off in a competitive matchup at Chase Center, where both teams battled for momentum throughout the night. The energy inside the arena reflected the intensity on the floor as neither side allowed the other to pull away comfortably.

Golden State’s offense was fueled by strong perimeter shooting, with key baskets falling at crucial moments to maintain their lead. The Valkyries’ backcourt played a central role in controlling the tempo, using quick ball movement to create scoring opportunities.

The Sparks leaned heavily on their frontcourt presence, attacking the paint and drawing fouls to stay within striking distance. Their ability to generate second-chance points kept the game tight deep into the fourth quarter.

In the final minutes, the Valkyries tightened up defensively, forcing turnovers and capitalizing on fast-break opportunities. Despite a late surge from Los Angeles, Golden State held steady at the free-throw line to protect their advantage.

The victory provided another statement win for the expansion Valkyries, who continue to prove they can hang with established franchises. For the Sparks, the result was a reminder of their potential but also of the consistency needed to close out tight road games.
