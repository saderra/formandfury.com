## FAA Gender and Mental Health Evaluation

As both a pilot and licensed clinical psychologist, I meet the WPATH criteria required to complete the FAA Gender Dysphoria Mental Health Status Report paperwork. I understand the critical importance of providing care and the responsibility mental health providers have in ensuring evaluations and therapy are compliant and available.

The evaluation process lasts up to 120 minutes, during which your mental health history and history of gender dysphoria will be thoroughly reviewed. The evaluation fee is {{ site.pricing.faa }}, which includes the assessment and paperwork completion. If you are experiencing financial hardship, please contact me to discuss other payment options.

## License and Experience

My background includes extensive work in LGBTQIA+ centers across the nation's most affirming regions. Through my PSYPACT license, I am able to provide accessible and affirming mental health care to individuals in states where LGBTQIA+ friendly services are under threat.

As one of the few licensed intersex psychologists in the nation, I bring a unique and deeply personal understanding to my practice. I have collaborated with leading neurologists, geneticists, endocrinologists, and human development experts, contributing to research on best practices, development, and parenting within the LGBTQIA+ community.
