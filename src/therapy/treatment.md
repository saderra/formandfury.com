### Depression
Living with depression can feel overwhelming and isolating, impacting daily functioning, relationships, and self-worth. In therapy, we’ll work together to understand the roots of your depression and develop strategies that foster resilience, coping, and renewed hope.

### Anxiety
Anxiety affects both the mind and body, creating a persistent sense of worry and unease. Together, we’ll explore ways to manage anxiety’s impact and restore a sense of calm and confidence, building tools to face life's challenges with greater ease.

### Trauma / PTSD
Trauma leaves deep, lasting effects that can disrupt well-being. I provide a safe space for healing, helping you navigate and make sense of past experiences while developing coping strategies to reclaim control and peace in your life.

### Abuse History
Surviving abuse often brings complex emotional and relational challenges. My approach centers on helping you process these experiences, regain a sense of safety, and rebuild self-esteem, fostering growth and resilience.

### Perfectionism
Perfectionism can lead to stress, self-criticism, and burnout. In therapy, we’ll address the pressures you face, explore healthier ways to measure success, and cultivate self-compassion, aiming for balance and fulfillment over perfection.

### Loneliness
Loneliness can feel deeply painful, especially in a world that values constant connection. We’ll work together to understand your unique needs, overcome barriers to connection, and build meaningful, supportive relationships.

### Family Conflict
Family dynamics can be a source of stress, especially when communication and understanding break down. I provide a supportive environment to help you navigate these conflicts, fostering clarity and healthy boundaries.

### Life Transitions
Navigating change, whether planned or unexpected, can bring a mix of emotions. Therapy provides guidance and support through these transitions, helping you embrace growth and adapt to new chapters with confidence and purpose.

### Religious Trauma
Religious trauma involves complex feelings around past religious experiences that may have caused harm. I offer a compassionate, non-judgmental space to process these experiences, helping you find healing, clarity, and personal peace.
